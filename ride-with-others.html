Mountain biking in South Australia

Ride with others

Clubs, group rides and bike parks for riding with mates, or finding new ones.

This is a starter page and it will grow. The easiest way into group riding is usually a club: most run social or member rides as well as races.

Group and social rides

  • Gravity Girls SAA not-for-profit club for women and girls. Member rides at levels 1 to 3, shuttle days, weekenders and come and try rides. Girls 10 and up are welcome (under 14s with an adult).
  • Human Projectiles Mountain Bike ClubSA club. Check their page for rides.
  • Mount Gambier MTBRiding and races in the South East (Facebook).
  • BIKE Melrose MTB ClubThe club in Melrose, in the Southern Flinders Ranges (Facebook).

More clubs, including the racing ones, are on Clubs and links.

Bike parks for a day out

  • Fox Creek Bike ParkSA's best-known trail park, with trails for every level, so a mixed group can all ride. Free and open dawn to dusk.
  • Eagle Mountain Bike ParkClose to the city, with technical runs and jumps. Better for groups with some trail experience.
  • Mount Crawford ForestNear the Barossa. Family-friendly cross-country right up to fast downhill.
  • MelroseA weekend trip with trails for every level.

Run a social ride or club that should be listed? Email support@mtbsa.com.au.
