Mountain Biking SA

New to racing?

Everything you need for your first mountain bike race in SA, for riders and the parents who drive them there.

The race types, in one line each

  • Cross-country (XC / XCO): a few laps of a loop with climbs and descents. The best place to start.
  • Short track (XCC): very short laps and quick races, about 20 minutes. Fun to watch.
  • Marathon (XCM): long distance or timed races, like 2 or 8 hours. Often done in teams.
  • Enduro: timed downhill sections, with untimed climbs between them.
  • Downhill (DH): one fast run down a steep track. Needs a full-face helmet and a downhill bike.
  • Come & try / skills days: not races. Relaxed rides or coaching, great for a first step.

Do I need a licence?

Most SA races need an AusCycling membership, which is the "licence" entry pages talk about. It covers your insurance while you ride.

  • If you're just trying racing once, many races let you buy a one-day or free-trial licence when you enter.
  • If you'll race a few times a year, a yearly membership is usually cheaper.
  • Each race on our calendar says which kind it needs. Bring your licence card or digital membership to sign-on.

How entering works

  1. Find a race on the calendar and tap it.
  2. Press Enter on the official page. Most SA races use a site called EntryBoss.
  3. Pick your category. That's usually your age group, based on how old you'll be on 31 December.
  4. Pay online and watch for the closing date. Entries often close a few days before the race.

What to bring

  • A bike in good working order (check the brakes and tyres the night before).
  • An Australian-standard helmet. Downhill needs a full-face helmet.
  • Gloves, water, snacks, a spare tube or plug kit, and a pump.
  • Your licence card or digital membership.
  • For the parents: a camp chair, sunscreen, a hat, warm layers and something to read. Race days can be long.
On the day: get there at least an hour early. Go to sign-on (sometimes called rego) to collect your number plate, then do a practice lap if the course is open.

What the colours on the calendar mean

  • Beginner Come & try rides, junior days and beginner series like Inside Line's First Descent downhill series. Start here.
  • Club Local club races. Open to everyone, with categories for every age and ability.
  • School Races you enter through your school.
  • State State series rounds and state championships. Most still have a Novice or Sport category for newer riders.
  • National National series and national championships. Great to watch, and a good reason for a group trip.

Never raced before?

Pick a Beginner or Club race. Most races also have a Novice category that you can enter once, just to try it. Everyone there was a beginner once.

This guide is general information. Every race has its own rules, so always read the official race page.
