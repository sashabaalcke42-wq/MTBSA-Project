# MTB SA roadmap

The plan for growing https://mtbsa.com.au, from launch week to the long run.
Each phase has a clear **start** and a clear **finish line**, so you always know when to move on.
Tick things off as you go. It's fine if a phase takes longer than planned.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

---

## Where we are now (done)

- [x] Site live at mtbsa.com.au on Cloudflare Pages
- [x] Race calendar with a page per race (`races.csv`)
- [x] New to racing guide, Where to ride, Links, Gear guide, Race day checklist
- [x] Google Search Console set up and sitemap sent
- [x] Visitor stats (PostHog) and the private stats dashboard (mtbsa-stats repo)
- [x] Site broadened from new and young riders to all SA riders: starter pages for experienced riders, group rides and clubs, and shops and skills clinics

## Phase 1: Tell people it exists (this week)

**Start:** 23 Nov 2026. **Finish line:** a contact email is on the site and at least 8 clubs or groups have been emailed.

1. [ ] Set up **support@mtbsa.com.au** for free with Cloudflare Email Routing (it forwards to your normal inbox).
2. [x] Put support@mtbsa.com.au on every page (shows in the footer).
3. [ ] Email SA clubs, trail crews and race organisers asking them to share or link the site (list and email draft are in the project files: `outreach/clubs-email-list.md`).
4. [ ] Post in SA MTB Facebook groups (check each group's rules first, and ask an admin if unsure).
5. [ ] Make an MTB SA Instagram and do a first post (the calendar is a good one).
6. [ ] Join or start a Strava club and share the link there.

## Phase 2: Follow up and fix (next 2 weeks, to about 7 Dec)

**Start:** when Phase 1 is done. **Finish line:** every email has had a reply or one polite follow-up, and at least 2 clubs link to the site.

1. [ ] Reply to anyone who wrote back. Add any races or fixes they send.
2. [ ] Send one short follow-up to anyone who didn't reply after a week. Only one.
3. [ ] Add a "Thanks to" or "Supported by" line for clubs that helped (if they're happy with it).
4. [ ] Check Search Console: are pages showing up in Google yet?

## Phase 3: More things worth reading (December to January)

**Start:** about 8 Dec. **Finish line:** 3 new pages live and the 2027 calendar is complete.

1. [ ] Fill in the full 2027 race calendar as clubs announce dates.
2. [ ] New page: **Best MTB trails near Adelaide** (top picks by skill level, builds on Where to ride).
3. [ ] New page: **Your first MTB race in SA, step by step** (a story-style walk through race day, links to New to racing).
4. [ ] New page: **Where to watch races** (good spots for families and friends at each venue).
5. [ ] Set up a free **Google Business Profile** for MTB SA (as an online service, no address shown).

## Phase 4: Keep it fresh (school term 1, Feb to April 2027)

**Start:** when the 2027 season kicks off. **Finish line:** a weekly habit that takes 20 minutes or less.

Every week (pick a day, e.g. Sunday):

- [ ] Check the stats dashboard: which pages people use most.
- [ ] Check Search Console: new searches people found you with, any errors.
- [ ] Add new races, mark changes, post "this weekend's races" on Instagram/Facebook.

Once a month:

- [ ] Add results links for finished races.
- [ ] Check that entry links and gear prices still work.

## Phase 5: Get more sites linking to you (2027)

**Start:** Term 1 2027. **Finish line:** 5 or more bike shops, clubs or trail sites link to mtbsa.com.au.

1. [ ] Ask Adelaide bike shops to link the calendar (or put up a small poster with a QR code).
2. [ ] Ask to be listed on club "links" pages and trail park pages.
3. [ ] Get the race calendar into school MTB groups (School Sport SA MTB, school MTB teams).

## Phase 6: Bigger ideas (later, only if it's going well)

No fixed dates. Pick one at a time.

- **Forums**: a place for SA riders to ask questions, find riding buddies and swap trail tips
- **Marketplace**: buy and sell used bikes, parts and gear within SA
- Grow the shops, clinics and group rides pages into fuller listings (ask shops and clubs to send their details)
- Go Australia-wide once SA is solid

- Results page or race reports for each round
- Club pages (one page per SA club with what they run)
- Email or calendar sign-up: "remind me before entries close"
- Trail conditions or closures page (with help from trail crews)
- Sponsorship from a local shop to cover costs (always say clearly when something is sponsored)

---

## How to use this plan

- Work on one phase at a time. Move on when you hit its finish line.
- If something isn't working (e.g. nobody replies), it's fine to skip it and try the next idea.
- Ask Claude to update this file whenever things change.
