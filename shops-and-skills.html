Mountain biking in South Australia

Shops and skills

Where to get your bike fixed, and where to get better at riding it.

This is a starter list, not a full directory. We haven't tested these ourselves and nobody pays to be here. Always check the business's own page for prices and times.

Bike shops and repairs

  • 99 BikesA chain with several stores around Adelaide that do servicing and repairs. Many of the gear guide picks come from here.
  • Krankin Bicycle TechA workshop in Lonsdale that services road, MTB, BMX and kids' bikes.
Tip: book a service a week or two before a big ride or race, as workshops get busy, and ask for a shop that knows suspension if your bike has it.

Skills clinics and coaching

  • Gravity Girls SA come and tryLevel 1 come and try rides for women and girls. Girls 10 and up are welcome (under 14s with an adult).
  • Mountain Bike Core Skills CourseA 5-hour small-group course at Craigburn Farm covering braking, climbing, descending and wheel lifts. Ages 16 and up, beginner to advanced. Booked through Adrenaline.
  • Head for the HillsRuns school holiday skills clinics for kids aged 10 to 14 at Cobbler Creek Recreation Park, with beginner and intermediate groups.

Clubs run junior and skills days too, like the AMBC Juniors Day. Look for Beginner events on the calendar.

Run a shop, workshop or clinic in SA? Email support@mtbsa.com.au and we'll take a look.
