Mountain biking in South Australia

Riding with kids

Kid-friendly trails around Adelaide, and easy ways for kids to start racing in SA.

Mountain biking is a great thing to do as a family, and South Australia has plenty of easy trails close to the city. Here's where to take kids riding, and how to get them into racing if they love it.

Kid-friendly trails around Adelaide

  • Cobbler Creek Recreation Park (northern suburbs): beginner loops and small jump tracks that suit kids, plus easy rides along the creek. Directions ↗
  • Shepherds Hill Recreation Park (about 11 km south): easy family loops. A relaxed first trail ride. Directions ↗
  • Belair National Park (about 25 minutes from the city): wide fire tracks that are great for little ones, and you can get there by train. Directions ↗
  • Kuitpo Forest (about 50 minutes south): easy marked loops and forest roads. Good for a longer family day out. Directions ↗
  • Fox Creek Bike Park (Cudlee Creek, Adelaide Hills): once they're confident, the green trails here are a great next step. Directions ↗

Going further? Melrose in the Southern Flinders Ranges has trails for every level and makes a great family weekend away. See Where to ride for more parks.

Tips for riding with kids

  • Get the size right. Kids should be able to put their feet down easily when stopped. A bike that's too big is harder to control.
  • Helmet every time, Australian-standard and fitted snugly. Gloves help a lot when they fall.
  • Keep it short and fun. Stop for snacks, play on the small jumps, and finish while they still want more.
  • Let them walk bits. Walking a scary section is totally normal, even for adults.
  • Check the weather. Summer heat and Total Fire Ban days can close parks. Our calendar shows the forecast for upcoming races.

When they want to race

Kids can start racing young, and SA has lots of easy ways in:

  • Club races have categories for every age, so kids race against others their age. Age is usually based on how old they'll be on 31 December.
  • Inside Line's First Descent series is a beginner downhill series on easier blue trails.
  • School Sport SA Mountain Bike Team Championship is a team race for Years 5 to 12, entered through school. Ask your school's sport teacher.
  • Gravity Girls SA runs free come & try rides for women and girls.

Look for Beginner, Club and School races on the calendar, and read New to racing? for licences and entering.
